## Overview

**Settings → Billing** shows your current plan, deflection usage for the month, and lets you upgrade, downgrade, or manage payment through Stripe's customer portal.

## Usage tracking

The usage bar shows deflections used against your plan's monthly limit. Once you cross 80% of your limit, a warning banner appears with a one-click upgrade to the next tier — so you see it coming instead of being surprised mid-month.

What happens when you reach 100% depends on your plan:

- **Standard (hard cap)** — AI auto-answering pauses until the next calendar-month reset and new questions route to your human queue instead. There is no Standard overage charge.
- **Pro (soft cap)** — answers keep going out. Usage past your included 3,000 deflections is billed at $5 per 100 (rounded up to the next 100), and the usage bar shows how far over you are.
- **Enterprise** — unlimited; there's no limit to reach.

## Upgrading or downgrading

Click **Upgrade to [plan]** on any plan card, or use the warning banner's upgrade shortcut. Upgrades take effect immediately — Stripe prorates the difference. Downgrades and cancellations go through **Manage subscription →**, which opens the Stripe customer portal; downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period.

## Trial

Hosted plans begin with a 14-day trial after checkout (card required). The trial banner shows days remaining; your card is charged automatically when the trial ends unless you cancel first.

## Free AI trial (no key required to start)

You don't need to add your own AI provider key to see answerLoops work. Every new org gets **5 free tickets** fully AI-processed — triaged and drafted, with automatic replies subject to your channel settings — on answerLoops' own key, so you can see the full pipeline before deciding whether to bring your own key. This is separate from (and much smaller than) your plan's monthly deflection limit above, and it's a one-time allowance, not something that resets.

Once you've used all 5, a banner appears across your dashboard asking you to add an AI provider key in **Settings → AI Model**; tickets continue to arrive, but AI triage and drafting pause until a key is added. This doesn't apply to self-hosted deployments, which always use your own configured key (or your own environment's key) from the start.

## Deleting your account

The workspace owner can delete the whole workspace from **Settings → Danger Zone**. You'll be asked to type the workspace name to confirm — this isn't a single click, since it's not reversible after the grace period below.

Deleting immediately:
- Cancels your Stripe subscription (no further charges)
- Signs everyone out and revokes access to the workspace

Your data (tickets, knowledge base, integrations, API keys) is kept for **30 days** in case this was a mistake. Logging back in during that window shows a **Restore my account** option instead of the normal dashboard. After 30 days, everything is permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.
