## Activity

When a customer replies on a ticket your team hasn't opened since, the dashboard shows an **Activity** panel above the stat cards. The panel starts collapsed. Select **Activity** to preview up to five tickets, most recent reply first. Each row shows the ticket number, summary, who wrote, which channel, and when. Select a row to open its ticket. The badge shows **99+** above 99 tickets. **View all** shows the exact total and opens the ticket list filtered to new activity and sorted by latest activity. Once a ticket is opened it leaves the panel, and the panel disappears when nothing is waiting.

When customer replies span multiple channels, the panel header displays channel chips showing where the replies are coming from (for example, "GitHub 8 • Discord 3 • Email 1"). Selecting a chip opens the tickets list filtered to that channel's unseen replies. If replies are spread across more than four channels, the top three are shown alongside an **Other** chip linking to the full list.

The bell in the top bar shows the same replies as notifications, and a push notification is sent if browser notifications are on. See [Know when a customer replies](/docs/product/tickets#know-when-a-customer-replies).

To clear the backlog, expand **Activity**, select **Mark all as seen**, and confirm. This clears new activity and unread customer-reply notifications for the tickets included in the displayed count, including those outside the preview. Replies that arrive after the dashboard loads stay unseen. The result tells you how many tickets were updated.

## Stat cards

| Card | What it shows | Opens |
|---|---|---|
| Auto-Answered | Every question the AI resolved automatically | Tickets filtered to auto-answered |
| Deflection rate | Deflected / Answered — your key efficiency metric | Analytics |
| Open | Questions that still need attention, with how many of them are in progress | Open tickets |
| Resolved | Completed support conversations | Resolved tickets |

Select any card to jump to the matching view. The ticket list shows the same tickets the card counts, and a "Filtered by" label appears above the list while a card filter is active. Pressing **Filter** keeps the card filter along with your status, priority, and category choices. Select **Clear card filter** to drop only the card filter.

## Needs attention

Below the stat cards, one list says what to do next. It starts with the counts that need a decision, most urgent first. A count of zero is left out:

| Row | What it counts | Opens |
|---|---|---|
| Tickets past the response target | Tickets past their configured response target | Tickets filtered to SLA breaches |
| AI drafts waiting for approval | Draft replies ready to review | Tickets filtered to pending drafts |
| Answers need a human review | Answers waiting for a human decision | Tickets filtered to needs review |

Under those, **Oldest open questions** lists up to five unresolved tickets, oldest first, so nothing sits unanswered. Select one to open it, or **View all open** for the full list. When there is nothing to do, the list reads **All caught up!**

If connected channels are set to manual review, a banner at the top of the dashboard says so and links to **Integrations**. It only shows on the dashboard. On the Integrations page each affected channel carries a **Manual review** tag instead.

<Callout type="info">
  The Deflection rate card uses the same figure as the Analytics page, which leaves out bug reports and feature requests. Auto-Answered counts every deflected ticket, so the two numbers won't always line up with each other.
</Callout>

Tickets marked `duplicate` are left out of every stat card, the deflection rate, category breakdown, and SLA figures, so a repeated question is only counted once. Mark a ticket as duplicate from its status form on the ticket page.

## Deflection rate

A deflection happens when the AI answers a question with confidence above your threshold (default 0.8) and the user doesn't escalate to a human. Higher = less manual support work.

Tickets classified as `bug` are excluded from this metric entirely — the knowledge base can't contain a fix that doesn't exist yet, so declining to auto-answer a bug report is correct behaviour, not a miss. Deflection rate reflects how well answerLoops handles questions the knowledge base can actually answer.

## Live updates

Tickets, draft availability, and team membership update while the dashboard is open. If the connection is interrupted, the page reconnects and refreshes. Returning to a background tab also refreshes its data.

Self-hosted operators using a pooled database connection should configure `DIRECT_DATABASE_URL` for live updates. See [environment variables](/docs/self-hosting/environment-variables).

## Navigation

The workspace sidebar groups pages by the job they support:

- **Monitor:** Dashboard and Analytics
- **Support:** Tickets
- **Improve:** Knowledge Base
- **Configure:** Billing, Integrations, and Settings

Related pages sit under the entry they belong to. A row of links at the top of the page moves between them: **Leads** next to **Tickets**, and **FAQ** and **Simulation** next to **Knowledge Base**. The sidebar entry stays highlighted on all of them, and each page keeps its own address (`/leads`, `/faq`, `/simulation`). Knowledge gaps is a tab on the Knowledge Base page.

On mobile, the same navigation is available from the menu button in the top bar.
