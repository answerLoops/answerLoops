Every question posted in a channel you have connected becomes a **ticket**: one record that follows the question from first contact to resolution. answerLoops reads the question, categorizes it, drafts an answer from your knowledge base, and keeps every reply, status change, and AI decision in one place, so your team never has to hunt through Discord, Slack, email, and GitHub separately.

## Find the tickets that need you

The **Tickets** page is a single inbox across every connected channel. Each row shows:

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **#** | Your workspace's ticket number |
| **Summary** | AI-written one-line summary of the question |
| **Source** | The platform the question came from |
| **Category** | The AI's classification (see [categories](#how-the-ai-handles-each-category)) |
| **Priority** | `critical`, `high`, `medium`, or `low` |
| **Status** | Where the ticket is in its lifecycle |
| **AI** | What the AI did with it (for example, AI needs review or AI approved) |
| **SLA** | Shows **SLA breach** in red when a response or resolution target has been missed; blank otherwise |
| **Author** | Who asked |
| **Created** | When the original message arrived |

The list shows the 50 newest tickets at a time. Select **Load more** at the bottom for older ones.

### Filter the list

Use the dropdowns at the top of the page, then press **Filter**:

- **Status:** Open, In Progress, Resolved, Closed, or Duplicate
- **Priority:** Critical, High, Medium, or Low
- **Category:** Bug, Feature Request, Documentation, How-to, or General Question

Filters stack, so you can combine all three. **Clear** resets the list.

Selecting a [dashboard stat card](/docs/product/dashboard#stat-cards) opens this page with a matching card filter (Auto-Answered, Needs Review, AI Drafts Pending, or SLA Breaches). A "Filtered by" label appears above the list and stays in place when you change the dropdowns and press **Filter**. Select **Clear card filter** to remove only the card filter.

### Where tickets come from

The **Source** badge identifies the platform. Badges are consistent across the app:

| Platform | Badge label | Color |
|---|---|---|
| Discord | Discord | Indigo |
| GitHub | GitHub | Gray |
| Slack | Slack | Green |
| Telegram | Telegram | Sky |
| Discourse | Discourse | Orange |
| Circle | Circle | Violet |
| Email | Email | Yellow |
| AI agents using the Agent API | Agent | Brand blue |

### Ticket statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `open` | New ticket, not yet handled |
| `in_progress` | A team member has replied, or moved it here |
| `resolved` | Answered; can be promoted to a knowledge base article |
| `closed` | Finished, no further action needed |
| `duplicate` | Same question as another ticket. Left out of dashboard counts, analytics, and SLA tracking so it doesn't inflate your numbers |

Sending the first reply on an `open` ticket moves it to `in_progress` automatically.

## Read a ticket

Select any row to open the ticket. The header gathers the key facts as badges: status, priority, category, AI status, and, when they apply, **SLA breached**, **Asked N×** (the same question has come up before), and the confidence result.

The page has two columns. On the left is the conversation: the **Original Message**, the **AI Draft Answer**, the **AI Confidence** card, earlier **Replies**, and the **Reply** form. On the right are the **Details**, **Related Questions**, **Update Status**, and **Activity** panels.

### Jump back to the original message

A link under the title takes you to the question on its own platform:

- GitHub: **View on GitHub ↗** (the issue or discussion)
- Discord: **View in Discord ↗**
- Slack: **View in Slack ↗**
- Telegram: **View in Telegram ↗**
- Discourse: **View in Discourse ↗**
- Circle: **View in Circle ↗**
- Email: shows the sender's address

If the original Discord message was deleted, the ticket says so and keeps its content.

### See related questions

**Related Questions** lists earlier tickets that are similar, with a match percentage. When an earlier ticket matches at 85% or more, the new ticket is flagged as a likely duplicate with the **Asked N×** badge. Open the match to reuse its answer, or mark the newer ticket `duplicate` from **Update Status**.

## Review an AI draft

For most tickets the AI has already written a draft by the time you open it. What happens next depends on how confident the AI reviewer was. A high-confidence draft can be sent to the customer automatically, when [Automatic Deflections](/docs/product/ai-deflection#automatic-deflections) is on for that channel. Anything else waits for you.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read the draft and its confidence">
    The **AI Draft Answer** panel shows the draft. The **AI Confidence** card underneath shows a percentage and a short explanation of the score.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what to do with it">
    - **Approve** posts the draft as it is to the source platform.
    - **Edit** opens the draft in a text field. **Save & post** sends your edited version.
    - **Dismiss** hides the draft without posting anything. The ticket's status does not change.
    - **Copy draft** copies the text, useful when you want to paste it somewhere yourself.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update the ticket">
    Under **Update Status**, pick the new status, enter your name, add optional resolution notes, and select **Update status**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
  If Automatic Deflections is off for the channel, nothing is sent to the customer even when the AI is confident. The confidence card says so, and the draft waits for you to approve it or reply by hand.
</Callout>

### See which articles the draft was given

Owners and admins also see a **Sources** panel under the draft. It lists the knowledge-base articles that were supplied as context for it, in order, and whether each is still current, has changed since, is unpublished or was deleted. See [Sources behind an answer](/docs/product/knowledge-base#sources-behind-an-answer).

### Reply in your own words

The **Reply** form sends a message back to the person who asked. Enter your name and your message (up to 2,000 characters). The button names the destination:

| Source | Button |
|---|---|
| GitHub | **Send reply to GitHub** |
| Discord | **Send reply to Discord** |
| Slack | **Send reply to Slack** |
| Telegram | **Send reply to Telegram** |
| Discourse | **Send reply to Discourse** |
| Google Chat | **Send reply to Google Chat** |
| Email | **Send reply by email** |
| Circle, Agent | **Save reply** (records the reply on the ticket) |

Replies go to the original thread on the source platform, not only into answerLoops. Circle tickets are ingest-only, so copy the answer into Circle yourself. For tickets opened by an AI agent there is no chat channel, so the reply is saved on the ticket for the agent to read through the API.

### Turn a good answer into knowledge

On a `resolved` or `closed` ticket that has an answer, the **Knowledge Base** panel offers **Promote to KB**. It turns the question and answer into a searchable article so the next person gets it instantly. Only owners and admins can promote. Once promoted, the panel links to the article instead.

### When a ticket has no draft

A ticket sent to a person without a draft shows a notice in place of the draft, and the notice says why.

| Notice | Why | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| You've reached this month's AI limit | Your plan's included AI usage for the month is spent. | Add your own AI provider in **Settings → AI Model**, or wait for the limit to reset next month. |
| You've reached your trial AI limit | The AI usage included in your trial is spent. | Add your own AI provider in **Settings → AI Model**. |
| AI drafting is paused because there's no active plan | The workspace has no active trial or plan. | Choose a plan in **Billing**. |
| We couldn't draft a reply for this ticket | AI was unavailable when the ticket arrived. | Nothing. A team member reviews the ticket. |
| Bug reports and feature requests aren't answered automatically | The ticket was classified as a bug report or feature request. These are never answered from the knowledge base, because the fix or feature may not exist yet. | Nothing. A team member reviews it. |
| This email is on hold because we couldn't verify the sender | An inbound email could not be matched to a verified sender, so it waits for a person. | Review it before replying. |
| No AI draft was generated for this ticket | An older ticket, created before the reason was recorded. | Nothing. |

<Callout type="info">
  Only the first three notices need anything from you. The others are expected behavior.
</Callout>

## How the AI handles each category

answerLoops classifies each ticket as it arrives. If classification is not possible (for example, no AI provider is available), the Category column stays blank and the ticket is handled as a general question.

| Category | Used for |
|---|---|
| `how_to` | Step-by-step usage questions |
| `documentation` | Requests to clarify or find docs |
| `bug` | Reports of broken behavior |
| `feature_request` | Suggestions for new functionality |
| `general_question` | Everything else that is a genuine question |

Category changes what the AI does next:

- **`how_to`, `documentation`, `general_question`:** answered from your knowledge base. A separate review grades the draft, and it is posted automatically only when confidence is high enough and Automatic Deflections is on. Otherwise it waits for a person.
- **`bug`, `feature_request`:** never answered from the knowledge base, because the fix or feature does not exist yet, and never graded for confidence. The AI instead works down this list and stops at the first match:
  1. **The same report already exists.** If a very similar earlier ticket exists, it replies that the team is already tracking it, with that ticket's number.
  2. **A workaround exists.** If the knowledge base has a relevant answer, it posts it clearly labeled as a workaround, not a fix.
  3. **Neither.** It acknowledges the report and routes the ticket straight to a person. No generated answer is posted.

<Callout type="info">
  These automated replies follow the same Automatic Deflections setting as every other reply. When it is off for a channel, they are saved as drafts for you to approve and nothing is posted.
</Callout>

## Export your tickets

Owners and admins can select **Export CSV** in the filter bar to download every ticket in the workspace as a comma-separated file. The export does not follow the filters on screen. It includes ID, status, priority, category, summary, content, author, created date, and resolved date.
