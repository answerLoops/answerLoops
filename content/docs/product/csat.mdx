Customer satisfaction scoring, usually shortened to **CSAT**, shows how the people you support rate the answers they get from the AI. Each rating is a number from 1 to 5, and the results roll up into an average and a breakdown you can read at a glance.

<Callout type="info">
  CSAT scoring is available on **Pro and Enterprise**, and in the self-hosted edition. On other plans the CSAT panel in Analytics shows an upgrade link instead of results.
</Callout>

## How customers rate an answer

When answerLoops answers a question automatically, it follows the answer with a short prompt asking the customer to rate it. The customer reacts to the prompt with a number emoji from 1️⃣ to 5️⃣.

- Ratings are recorded from reactions on **Discord** and **Slack**.
- The prompt appears after answers the AI posted on its own. Drafts your team approves and sends by hand do not get one.
- Each rating is attached to the ticket it belongs to and counted in your workspace totals.

## Read the results

Open **Analytics** and find the **Customer satisfaction (CSAT)** panel. It shows:

| Figure | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| **Avg. rating** | The mean of all submitted ratings, out of 5 |
| **Total ratings** | How many responses you have received |
| **Distribution** | A bar for each score from 5★ down to 1★, showing how many customers chose it |

Until the first rating arrives the panel shows "No ratings yet". An empty panel is not a good or bad result, just an absence of data.

## Interpret the numbers

- **Read the count with the average.** A handful of ratings can swing the result a lot, so a 4.8 from five responses means less than a 4.3 from two hundred.
- **Use low ratings as a starting point.** Open the ticket behind a low score, read the answer the customer received, and check whether the knowledge base behind it was missing or out of date. A score points you at an answer to look into; it does not diagnose the cause.

### CSAT is not the same as thumbs up or down

CSAT is a customer's rating of the answer they received. Separately, an answer can be marked good or bad with thumbs up or down, either from the buttons on the ticket page or by a 👍 or 👎 reaction on the answer. That feedback is a different measure and appears in **Answer quality by category**, not in the CSAT panel.
