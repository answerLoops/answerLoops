The FAQ Generator turns what your team resolved this week into a structured FAQ, written as clean question and answer pairs and grouped by topic. The result reflects what your community is actually asking, not what you assumed they would ask when you wrote your original docs. Open **FAQ** from the **Improve** group in the sidebar. It is available on every plan.

## Generate a FAQ

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Open **FAQ** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Click **Regenerate FAQ**. If you have never generated one, the page shows **Generate from this week's tickets** instead.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Wait for the button to finish **Generating**. The new FAQ replaces the one on the page, and the line above it shows the date and how many tickets it covers.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Review the text and copy it to your docs site, a Notion page, a Discord `#faq` channel, or your GitHub wiki.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Generating is always something you start yourself. answerLoops does not publish the FAQ anywhere, so you stay in control of where it goes.

## What goes into it

The generator looks at every ticket marked **resolved** or **closed** in the last 7 days. Open and in-progress tickets are left out. It groups those tickets by category, then asks the AI to write a clean FAQ with a heading for each category and clear, self-contained answers.

For each ticket, the AI sees a short summary of the question and the resolution notes your team wrote. A ticket resolved without notes is treated as "resolved by team", which gives the AI little to work with. The more specific your resolution notes, the more useful the FAQ.

The output has no ticket numbers or internal references, so it is safe to paste into a public place. Read it through first anyway, because it is AI-written. Several tickets that ask the same thing can be merged into one entry.

## FAQ history and API access

Every time you generate a FAQ, answerLoops saves a snapshot with the full text, the number of tickets included, and the calendar week it was generated in (Monday to Sunday). The FAQ page always shows the most recent snapshot. Your own tools can read the same latest FAQ through the [Agent API](/docs/integrations/agent-api) or the `get_faq` tool in [MCP](/docs/integrations/mcp).

## Troubleshooting

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="The FAQ says there were no resolved tickets">
    If nothing was resolved or closed in the last 7 days, generating still saves a snapshot, but it only reads "No resolved tickets this week." Go to **Tickets**, filter by status, and confirm some were resolved recently, then generate again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The button finishes but nothing changes">
    The most common cause is that no AI provider is available. Check your provider in **Settings → AI Model**, then try again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The FAQ is too long or too broad">
    The generated text has a length limit, so it stays a digest rather than a full manual. To keep it focused, close duplicate tickets as duplicates rather than resolving them separately, and edit the output after generating.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
