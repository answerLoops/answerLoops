Bring your colleagues into the workspace so they can answer tickets, review AI drafts, and keep the knowledge base current. Owners and admins manage the whole team from **Settings → Team**: invite people, see who has access, and remove or hand over ownership when things change. Team management is included on every plan.

## Invite a teammate

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the Team tab

Go to **Settings → Team**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Enter their details

Under **Invite a teammate**, enter the person's email address and choose **Member** or **Admin**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Send the invite

Click **Send invite**. The invitation link is copied to your clipboard automatically, so you can paste it straight into a message. If the person is already a member, you will see a message saying so instead.

</Step>
<Step>

### Ask them to accept

Ask the recipient to open the link and sign in with the invited email address. They join as soon as they select **Accept invitation**.

</Step>
</Steps>

Where email delivery is configured, answerLoops also emails the invitation. Invitations are valid for 7 days, and sending a new invitation to the same address replaces the earlier pending one. If you lose the link, copy it again with **Copy link** under **Pending Invites**.

The new person appears in **Members** after accepting, and the list updates on its own, without a refresh.

## Choose the right role

| Role | Purpose | Who can grant it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Owner | Holds workspace ownership and can transfer it to another member. | Transfer from the current owner only |
| Admin | Manages workspace settings and membership. | Owners |
| Member | Works with support tickets and workspace content. | Owners and admins |

Give administrative access only to people who need to manage the workspace. Invitations cannot create another owner; use ownership transfer instead.

## Manage invitations

**Pending Invites** appears whenever invitations are waiting. Each row shows the email address, the role, and the expiry date.

- **Copy link:** copies the invitation link so you can resend it.
- **Revoke:** cancels an invitation that should no longer grant access.

## Remove a member

Find the person in **Members** and choose **Remove**. Their membership is removed straight away. A few rules apply:

- The owner cannot be removed. Transfer ownership first when the current owner is leaving.
- You cannot remove yourself.
- Only an owner can remove an admin.

## Transfer ownership

<Steps>
<Step>

### Choose the new owner

As the current owner, select **Transfer Ownership** beside another member. The button appears only when the workspace has at least one other member.

</Step>
<Step>

### Confirm

Check the name in the dialog, then select **Yes, transfer ownership**.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn">
  You become a regular member and lose owner privileges. This cannot be undone unless the new owner transfers ownership back to you.
</Callout>

## If an invitation does not work

<Accordions>
<Accordion title="The link says it is invalid or already used">
The invitation was already accepted or revoked. Check **Pending Invites**; if it is gone, send a new one.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="The link says it has expired">
Invitations last 7 days. Revoke the old one if it is still listed, then send a new invitation.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="The page says you are signed in with a different email">
The invitation only works for the address it was sent to. Sign out, then sign in with the invited email. If the address was wrong, revoke the invitation and send a new one with the correct address and role.
</Accordion>
</Accordions>
