---
title: Product feedback
description: Share feedback with the answerLoops team and follow product updates without leaving the dashboard.
---

The feedback widget sits in the bottom-right corner of every dashboard page, in every workspace. Collapsed, it is a small pill with the answerLoops logo and a live count of approved feedback. Select it to open the panel.

## Feedback tab

The Feedback tab lists feedback from people across answerLoops. Each entry shows who it is from and any reply from the answerLoops team.

To leave your own:

1. Write your feedback in the box at the bottom of the panel. It needs at least 35 characters and can be up to 500. Links are not allowed.
2. Optionally tick **Post anonymously** to hide your workspace name. Entries otherwise show the name of your workspace.
3. Select **Submit**.

New feedback is reviewed before it is shown to other people. Until it is approved, only you see it, marked **Pending review**. You can send up to five entries an hour.

## Updates tab

The Updates tab is a feed of what the answerLoops team has recently shipped, newest first. The number beside the tab is how many updates are posted.

## Live updates

The panel and the count on the collapsed pill update on their own when feedback is approved, replied to, or a new update is posted. You do not need to refresh the page.

## Availability

The feedback board is part of the answerLoops-hosted product. It collects feedback on answerLoops itself, so self-hosted deployments do not show it.

When a workspace is permanently deleted, the feedback its members submitted is deleted with it.
