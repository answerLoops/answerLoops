title: Integrations
description: Connect answerLoops to the channels your community uses, the knowledge you already have, and the AI provider you prefer.
icon: Plug
---

Pick the pieces you need. Most teams connect one channel, point answerLoops at their documentation, and leave the AI provider on its default. Everything else is optional.

## Where to start

| If you want to... | Start here |
| --- | --- |
| Answer questions in a chat community | [Discord](/docs/integrations/discord) or [Slack](/docs/integrations/slack) |
| Answer questions on a forum | [Discourse](/docs/integrations/discourse) or [Circle](/docs/integrations/circle) |
| Answer questions on your website | [Chat widget](/docs/product/chat-widget) |
| Teach answerLoops from your docs | [GitHub](/docs/integrations/github) or [Notion](/docs/integrations/notion) |
| Use your own AI account | [OpenAI](/docs/integrations/openai), [Anthropic](/docs/integrations/anthropic), or [Ollama](/docs/integrations/ollama) for fully local models |
| Let an AI agent use your knowledge | [MCP server](/docs/integrations/mcp) |
