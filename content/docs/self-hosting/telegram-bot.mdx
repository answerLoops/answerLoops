Telegram needs almost no operator setup. There is no shared bot and no platform-wide credential: each organization creates its own bot with [@BotFather](https://t.me/BotFather) and pastes the token into **Integrations → Telegram**, exactly as on managed cloud. So this page is short on purpose. It covers the only part that belongs to whoever runs the deployment, and sends you to the [Telegram integration guide](/docs/integrations/telegram) for everything else.

When you are done, Telegram can reach your deployment, and your team can connect a bot.

## Operator checklist

<Steps>
  <Step title="Make the app reachable over public HTTPS">
    Telegram delivers messages by calling your app, so the app needs an address Telegram can reach on the public internet over HTTPS. Set `AUTH_URL` to that address (for example `https://support.yourcompany.com`). Addresses like `localhost` or private hostnames are refused by Telegram.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Only if you need to: set a separate webhook address">
    `TELEGRAM_WEBHOOK_BASE_URL` overrides `AUTH_URL` for the Telegram webhook only. You need it just when the app is reached at a different address than the one Telegram should call, for example when you develop on `localhost` and expose the webhook through a tunnel such as ngrok. Leave it unset for a normal deployment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let your team connect their bot">
    Nothing else is required. Your team creates the bot, pastes its token into **Integrations → Telegram** and clicks **Connect**, and the webhook registers itself at `<your-app-url>/api/telegram/webhook`. Follow the [integration guide's setup](/docs/integrations/telegram#setup) for BotFather, chat IDs and group privacy settings.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Environment variable

| Variable | Required | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `AUTH_URL` | Yes | Your public app URL. It is the address Telegram is told to call |
| `TELEGRAM_WEBHOOK_BASE_URL` | No | Overrides `AUTH_URL` for the Telegram webhook only. Leave it unset unless you use a tunnel |

<Callout type="warn">
  Do not point `AUTH_URL` at a tunnel just to test Telegram locally. `AUTH_URL` also controls where Google sign-in returns, so changing it breaks login. Set `TELEGRAM_WEBHOOK_BASE_URL` to the tunnel address instead.
</Callout>

If the bot connects but the webhook did not register, set `AUTH_URL` (or `TELEGRAM_WEBHOOK_BASE_URL`) to a public HTTPS address and click **Register webhook** on the Telegram card. The [troubleshooting section](/docs/integrations/telegram#troubleshooting) of the integration guide covers a bot that connects but never replies.
