Go from sign-up to a first AI-drafted answer on the hosted version of answerLoops. Most of the wait is your first knowledge import finishing.

<Callout type="info" title="What you need">
  A Google account to sign in, nothing else to pay for: the 14-day trial needs no card, a page or file with your current help content, and permission to install or configure an integration on the platform you want to connect.
</Callout>

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Start the 14-day trial

    Open [answerLoops pricing](https://answerloops.com/pricing) and select **Start 14-day free trial**. Sign in with **Google**. If you are new, a short step follows: tick the box to accept the terms of service, optionally say it is OK to email you product updates, and select **Start my free trial**. There is no card and no checkout, and your trial starts right then. If you already have an account, signing in simply takes you back to your dashboard.

    | Plan | Monthly | Annual, per month | Deflections per month |
    |---|---|---|---|
    | Standard | $49 | $39 | 500 |
    | Pro | $149 | $119 | 3,000 |
    | Enterprise | $499 | $399 | Unlimited |

    Every feature is unlocked for the whole trial, with the same access as Enterprise. When the 14 days end you choose a plan from the table above and add a card to continue. AI usage is included, so you can seed your knowledge base and see answers drafted without a model-provider key. Connect your own provider in **Settings → AI Model** any time to remove the limit; your provider then bills that usage directly. See [Billing plans](/docs/reference/billing-plans) and [Billing](/docs/product/billing) for how allowances and included usage work.

    **What you should see:** after you sign in you land in a short setup wizard with four steps: **Workspace**, **Connect**, **Seed KB**, and **Go live**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Name your workspace

    Enter a **Workspace name**, such as your community or product name, and select **Continue →**. Your team uses this name to recognize the workspace.

    **What you should see:** the wizard moves on to **Connect your community**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Connect a channel

    The wizard offers **Discord**, **Slack**, **GitHub**, and **Website Widget**. Pick one and follow the prompts, or select **Skip for now — connect later in Integrations**. Every other channel is connected from **Integrations** in the sidebar, where each channel has its own tab.

    - [Discord](/docs/integrations/discord) and [Slack](/docs/integrations/slack)
    - [Discourse](/docs/integrations/discourse) and [Circle](/docs/integrations/circle)
    - [GitHub](/docs/integrations/github)
    - [Telegram](/docs/integrations/telegram), [Email](/docs/integrations/email), and [Google Chat](/docs/integrations/google-chat)
    - [Website widget](/docs/product/chat-widget)

    You need permission to install or configure the integration on that platform. Select the channels, spaces, or repositories answerLoops should monitor.

    <Screenshot
      shot="integrations"
      caption="The Integrations page has a tab for each channel and service. A connected channel shows its status and the servers or spaces it monitors."
    />

    **What you should see:** the channel's tab on **Integrations** shows it as connected, with the channels or spaces you chose.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Add knowledge

    In the **Seed your knowledge base** step, choose **Upload file** (PDF, DOCX, MD, TXT, or CSV, up to 10 MB) or **Crawl a URL** (a public docs page or site root, then **Import URL**). You can also skip it and add content later from **Knowledge Base** in the sidebar, where you can also connect GitHub or Notion content.

    Wait for the import to complete. The wizard reports how many articles or chunks were added.

    <Screenshot
      shot="knowledge-base"
      caption="The Knowledge Base page: add an article, write an answer by hand, and connect a GitHub repository."
    />

    Notion content is initially unpublished. Review it and publish the intended material before using it for customer answers. See [Knowledge Base](/docs/product/knowledge-base).

    **What you should see:** a confirmation with the number of articles added, then the **You're all set!** screen. Select **Go to dashboard →**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Inspect a draft

    Ask a question covered by the imported content in your connected channel. It appears as a ticket under **Tickets**. Open the ticket and check the draft, including whether it answers the question and matches the source.

    <Screenshot
      shot="ticket-detail"
      caption="A ticket with the original message, the drafted answer, and the confidence score from the reviewing AI."
    />

    Automatic replies are off by default. A question appearing in the queue without a posted reply can be expected behavior. If no draft appears, open the ticket: a notice where the draft would be says why, for example that you've reached your AI limit.

    **What you should see:** a ticket with your question, a draft answer, and a confidence panel. A low-confidence draft is held for a person instead.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Choose reply settings

    Open the channel's tab on **Integrations** and turn on **Automatic Deflections** for the channels you are ready to automate. A draft must pass the configured confidence threshold (0.8 by default) before it qualifies. If the source material is missing or inaccurate, correct it before changing the threshold.

    Invite teammates through [Settings → Team](/docs/product/team), and review [AI Model](/docs/product/ai-config) and [Billing Plans](/docs/reference/billing-plans) before launch.

    **What you should see:** the channel's tab shows Automatic Deflections as on, and qualifying questions are answered without waiting for a person.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn" title="Test before you automate">
  A confidence score is an AI assessment, not a guarantee. Review several drafts from real questions before turning on Automatic Deflections.
</Callout>
