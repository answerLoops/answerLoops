## Plans

Pricing is deflection-based, not per-seat. Every hosted plan includes the full AI pipeline — higher tiers add scale, insight, and support.

| Plan | Price (monthly) | Price (annual, per mo) | Deflections/mo | Beyond the limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Standard | $49 | $39 (20%+ off) | 500 | Hard cap — auto-answering pauses |
| Pro | $149 | $119 (20%+ off) | 3,000 | Soft cap — keeps answering, $5 per 100 over |
| Enterprise | $499 | $399 (20%+ off) | Unlimited | — |

There is no free tier. Every plan starts with a 14-day trial — see below.

Annual billing saves at least 20%. The annual charges are **$468 Standard**, **$1,428 Pro**, and **$4,788 Enterprise**, paid once per year. The equivalent monthly amounts in the table are not monthly installments.

## What counts as a deflection

An automated channel answer counts when it meets the configured confidence threshold without human intervention. High-confidence standalone answers generated through MCP or the Agent API also count. Drafts sent to a human for review do not count.

Allowances reset each **calendar month**, including on annual subscriptions.

## Trial

Pick a plan at signup and it's fully unlocked for 14 days (card required) — every feature and the deflection limit of the tier you chose, not a reduced trial-only allowance. Cancel any time before the trial ends and you won't be charged. If the trial ends without a valid payment method, access stops immediately — there's no free tier to drop back to.

## Included AI usage

Every hosted plan includes AI usage, so no AI provider key is required: triage, drafting and knowledge-base processing run on answerLoops' own models up to a limit, for the whole 14-day trial and then each calendar month on a paid plan (annual plans included). Answers and knowledge-base processing have separate limits. A dashboard banner appears at 80% of either, and once one is used up that part of AI pauses until you add your own provider in **Settings → AI Model** (or, on a paid plan, until the next month starts; the trial's usage doesn't reset). An OpenAI key removes both limits; another provider removes the answers limit, and an OpenAI embedding key removes the knowledge-base one. Doesn't apply to self-hosted deployments. See [Billing](/docs/product/billing).

## What each plan includes

The [pricing page](https://answerloops.com/pricing) has the full feature comparison and is the one place it is kept up to date. The differences that affect day-to-day use are the deflection allowance above and how the limit behaves, described next.

## Approaching or hitting your limit

Once an org crosses 80% of its monthly deflection allowance, an in-app warning banner appears on **Settings → Billing** with a one-click upgrade to the next tier.

What happens at the limit depends on the plan's cap:

- **Standard (hard cap)** — AI auto-answering pauses until the next calendar-month reset once 500 deflections are used, and new questions route straight to the human queue instead. There is no Standard overage charge.
- **Pro (soft cap)** — answers keep going out past 3,000 deflections. Usage beyond the included amount is billed at $5 per 100 (rounded up to the nearest 100). The Billing page shows how far over the plan the org is.
- **Enterprise** — unlimited; there is no cap to reach.

## Self-hosting

The core platform is AGPL-3.0 licensed. Self-hosted deployments have every feature unlocked, no deflection limit, and no billing integration required — see [Production Setup Guide](/docs/self-hosting/production).
