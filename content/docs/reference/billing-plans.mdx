answerLoops Cloud has three plans, priced by how many questions the AI answers for you rather than by seat. This page lists what each plan costs, how many answers it includes, and what happens when you reach the limit, so you can pick a plan before you start a trial.

A **deflection** is a question the AI answered on its own, without a person stepping in. Every plan includes the full AI pipeline; higher tiers add scale, insight and support.

## What counts as a deflection

An automated channel answer counts when it meets the configured confidence threshold without human intervention. High-confidence standalone answers generated through MCP or the Agent API also count. Drafts sent to a human for review do not count.

Allowances reset each **calendar month**, including on annual subscriptions.

## Start with the trial

Pick a plan at signup and it is fully unlocked for 14 days (card required). You get every feature and the deflection limit of the tier you chose, not a reduced trial-only allowance.

- Cancel any time before the trial ends and you won't be charged.
- If the trial ends without a valid payment method, access stops immediately. There is no free tier to drop back to.

## Included AI usage

Every hosted plan includes AI usage, so no AI provider key is required: triage, drafting and knowledge-base processing run on answerLoops' own models up to a limit, for the whole 14-day trial and then each calendar month on a paid plan (annual plans included). Answers and knowledge-base processing have separate limits.

A dashboard banner appears at 80% of either. Once you reach one of them, that part of AI pauses until you add your own provider in **Settings → AI Model** (or, on a paid plan, until the next month starts; the trial's usage doesn't reset). An OpenAI key removes both limits; another provider removes the answers limit, and an OpenAI embedding key removes the knowledge-base one. This doesn't apply to self-hosted deployments. See [Billing](/docs/product/billing).

## Approaching or hitting your limit

Once an org crosses 80% of its monthly deflection allowance, an in-app warning banner appears on **Settings → Billing** with a one-click upgrade to the next tier. What happens at the limit depends on the plan's cap.

<Accordions type="single">
  <Accordion title="Standard (hard cap)">
    AI auto-answering pauses until the next calendar-month reset once 500 deflections are used, and new questions route straight to the human queue instead. There is no Standard overage charge.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Pro (soft cap)">
    Answers keep going out past 3,000 deflections. Usage beyond the included amount is billed at $5 per 100 (rounded up to the nearest 100). The Billing page shows how far over the plan the org is.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Enterprise">
    Unlimited; there is no cap to reach.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>

## Self-hosting

The core platform is AGPL-3.0 licensed. Self-hosted deployments have every feature unlocked, no deflection limit, and no billing integration required. See the [self-hosting production guide](/docs/self-hosting/production).
