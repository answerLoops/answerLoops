Knowledge Gaps now groups repeated questions, answerLoops records which articles ground your answers, and connecting Discord leads straight into choosing channels. Tickets also say plainly why they have no AI draft.

### Upgrade steps
- Four database migrations (0055 to 0058) run automatically at boot. They add article usage counts, a sync job lease, retrieval and review limits, and the reason a ticket has no AI draft. Nothing needs to be run by hand.

### New
- **Repeated questions on Knowledge Gaps** — tickets asking the same question are folded into one row with an "Asked N times" badge, most-asked first. [Docs](https://answerloops.com/docs/product/knowledge-gaps) ([#388](https://github.com/answerLoops/answerLoops/issues/388), [@NathanTarbert](https://github.com/NathanTarbert))
- **Article usage tracking** — answerLoops now counts how often each article grounds an answer (widget replies, ticket drafts, Discord /ask and Agent API answers). Counts are stored as a number per article per day, with no question, visitor or ticket attached, and are removed with the article or workspace. [Docs](https://answerloops.com/docs/product/knowledge-base) ([#389](https://github.com/answerLoops/answerLoops/issues/389), [@NathanTarbert](https://github.com/NathanTarbert))
- **Channel selection after connecting Discord** — a newly connected server opens its channel picker automatically, shows "Needs channels" until you save at least one, and leaves a "Connect channels" button if you close it. [Docs](https://answerloops.com/docs/integrations/discord) ([#385](https://github.com/answerLoops/answerLoops/issues/385), [@NathanTarbert](https://github.com/NathanTarbert))

### Improved
- Tickets without an AI draft now say why: bug reports and feature requests are left for a person by design, unverified email senders are held for review, and a reached AI limit or missing plan links to the right place. Older tickets get a neutral note. ([#407](https://github.com/answerLoops/answerLoops/issues/407), [@NathanTarbert](https://github.com/NathanTarbert))
- The OpenAPI spec and the OAuth protected-resource metadata now advertise your public address, taken from `APP_URL` and then your proxy's forwarded host, instead of the internal one. ([#400](https://github.com/answerLoops/answerLoops/issues/400), [#402](https://github.com/answerLoops/answerLoops/issues/402), [@NathanTarbert](https://github.com/NathanTarbert))
- Article limits and prompt formatting are applied the same way everywhere articles are added or answers are generated. ([#394](https://github.com/answerLoops/answerLoops/issues/394), [@NathanTarbert](https://github.com/NathanTarbert))
- Self-hosting sign-in docs now describe Google as the only sign-in method, and the README is a short overview with one-command setup. ([#382](https://github.com/answerLoops/answerLoops/issues/382), [#408](https://github.com/answerLoops/answerLoops/issues/408), [@NathanTarbert](https://github.com/NathanTarbert))

### Fixed
- Connecting Discord, Slack, Gmail or Outlook showed no result: no success message, no channel picker, and a failure looked like nothing had happened. The routes now land on the Integrations page and show the outcome. ([#384](https://github.com/answerLoops/answerLoops/issues/384), [@NathanTarbert](https://github.com/NathanTarbert))
- The Slack logo on the homepage, sign-in page and blog covers was drawn as scattered shapes. It now shows the real mark. ([#409](https://github.com/answerLoops/answerLoops/issues/409), [@NathanTarbert](https://github.com/NathanTarbert))

### Dependency updates
- The AI SDK moved to v7 with its provider packages at v4, Stripe packages to their new majors, and CopilotKit with the AG-UI client, plus routine minor and patch updates. ([#395](https://github.com/answerLoops/answerLoops/issues/395), [#396](https://github.com/answerLoops/answerLoops/issues/396), [#398](https://github.com/answerLoops/answerLoops/issues/398), [#403](https://github.com/answerLoops/answerLoops/issues/403))
