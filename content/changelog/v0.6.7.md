Tickets now keep the whole conversation on every channel, knowledge-base articles can be verified and traced to the answers they informed, the chat widget ships as an npm package, and the hosted AI models move to the newer generation.

### Upgrade steps
- Database migrations `0061` through `0066` run automatically when the app starts. There is no separate migration step. They add article verification, answer-to-article references, ticket conversation messages, and storage that later releases will use to check how well answers are supported by their sources. Nothing reads or writes that storage yet, and no new environment variable or service is needed.

### New
- **Verify knowledge-base articles.** Owners and admins can mark an article as verified, with an optional note. Each article shows "Never verified", "Verified on a date by a person", or "Changed since last verified". [Docs](https://answerloops.com/docs/product/knowledge-base) by [@NathanTarbert](https://github.com/NathanTarbert) ([#488](https://github.com/answerLoops/answerLoops/issues/488))
- **Article history.** Each article now has a history of its changes in the dashboard. by [@NathanTarbert](https://github.com/NathanTarbert) ([#490](https://github.com/answerLoops/answerLoops/issues/490))
- **See which articles informed an answer.** A Sources panel under a ticket's AI draft lists the articles supplied as context, marked Current, Changed since, Unpublished or Deleted. An article shows which answers it was supplied to. This covers the website chat widget, Discord `/ask`, `generate_answer` for the Agent API and MCP, and ticket drafts. [Docs](https://answerloops.com/docs/product/knowledge-base) by [@NathanTarbert](https://github.com/NathanTarbert) ([#491](https://github.com/answerLoops/answerLoops/issues/491), [#492](https://github.com/answerLoops/answerLoops/issues/492), [#493](https://github.com/answerLoops/answerLoops/issues/493))
- **Whole conversation on a ticket.** A ticket keeps every message after the opening one, labeled Customer, Staff or Maintainer, in a Conversation section. On GitHub, comments on an issue or discussion join that issue's ticket instead of opening one ticket per comment, and a Telegram reply joins the ticket it answers. [Docs](https://answerloops.com/docs/product/tickets) by [@NathanTarbert](https://github.com/NathanTarbert) ([#510](https://github.com/answerLoops/answerLoops/issues/510))
- **answerLoops's own replies in the conversation.** The automatic answer, the "a team member will follow up" note, the duplicate-ticket notice and approved AI drafts now appear in the Conversation, labeled "answerLoops". Older GitHub comment tickets stay separate. by [@NathanTarbert](https://github.com/NathanTarbert) ([#518](https://github.com/answerLoops/answerLoops/issues/518), [#516](https://github.com/answerLoops/answerLoops/issues/516))
- **Chat widget as an npm package.** `@answerloops/widget` mounts the widget from your own instance without a script tag, with a React component for Next.js layouts. The script-tag install is unchanged. [Docs](https://answerloops.com/docs/product/chat-widget) by [@NathanTarbert](https://github.com/NathanTarbert) ([#486](https://github.com/answerLoops/answerLoops/issues/486))
- **Changelog page.** A changelog that mirrors GitHub releases, linked from the docs header and the dashboard sidebar on the hosted site. by [@NathanTarbert](https://github.com/NathanTarbert) and [@lizuyi-6](https://github.com/lizuyi-6) ([#505](https://github.com/answerLoops/answerLoops/issues/505), [#514](https://github.com/answerLoops/answerLoops/issues/514))
- **Newer hosted AI models.** `gpt-6.1-terra` and `gpt-6-luna` are priced for included usage and offered in the model lists in Settings and Simulation. Defaults are unchanged. by [@NathanTarbert](https://github.com/NathanTarbert) ([#504](https://github.com/answerLoops/answerLoops/issues/504))

### Improved
- Unknown URLs now return a real 404 with a "Page not found" page instead of redirecting to sign-in. Known routes behave as before. by [@NathanTarbert](https://github.com/NathanTarbert) ([#506](https://github.com/answerLoops/answerLoops/issues/506))
- The sign-in page is no longer indexed, and its terms and privacy links point at the marketing site on the hosted deployment. Self-hosted installs keep relative links. by [@NathanTarbert](https://github.com/NathanTarbert) ([#519](https://github.com/answerLoops/answerLoops/issues/519))
- The Deflection Rate card on the dashboard is readable again. by [@NathanTarbert](https://github.com/NathanTarbert) ([#466](https://github.com/answerLoops/answerLoops/issues/466))
- The Automatic Deflections help text in Settings is corrected. by [@NathanTarbert](https://github.com/NathanTarbert) ([#473](https://github.com/answerLoops/answerLoops/issues/473), [#474](https://github.com/answerLoops/answerLoops/issues/474))
- The self-hosting guide, the product guide, and the integration pages are rewritten, with a complete variable list, diagrams and screenshots. There is also a new guide on testing AI code without an API key. by [@NathanTarbert](https://github.com/NathanTarbert) ([#465](https://github.com/answerLoops/answerLoops/issues/465), [#467](https://github.com/answerLoops/answerLoops/issues/467), [#470](https://github.com/answerLoops/answerLoops/issues/470), [#471](https://github.com/answerLoops/answerLoops/issues/471), [#499](https://github.com/answerLoops/answerLoops/issues/499), [#502](https://github.com/answerLoops/answerLoops/issues/502))

### Fixed
- Importing the same document more than once left several identical articles, and one article could fill every context slot. File uploads, URL imports and manual articles now skip an exact copy, and imports report how many sections were skipped. by [@NathanTarbert](https://github.com/NathanTarbert) ([#515](https://github.com/answerLoops/answerLoops/issues/515))
- An empty reply from the AI during triage or grading crashed with a raw error. It now counts as a failed assessment, routes to human review, and `generate_answer` returns the drafted answer. by [@NathanTarbert](https://github.com/NathanTarbert) ([#496](https://github.com/answerLoops/answerLoops/issues/496))
- The Circle, Discourse and dual-agent landing pages sent signed-out visitors to login. They are public again. by [@NathanTarbert](https://github.com/NathanTarbert) ([#494](https://github.com/answerLoops/answerLoops/issues/494))
